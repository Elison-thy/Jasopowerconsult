About the company

Practical electrical and solar solutions, rooted in hard work.

JASO Power Consult Limited began in 2016 as an electrical shop at Caravan Building, Plot 2B, Maruzi Road, opposite the UMEME Office in Lira City, Uganda.

The name “JASO” reflects the Kiswahili word for “sweat” — the clear, salty liquid produced through hard work. The name also comes from Jasper Ojok, the company’s Managing Director, Jasper George Ojok. It expresses the founder’s belief that hard work is essential to achieving what one desires.

From its beginnings as an electrical shop, the company developed its retail and technical services, later establishing JASO Electric Mart in Lira and expanding to Gulu in 2022.

Our journey

From a local electrical shop to a growing technical services company.

Key milestones from the JASO Power Consult Limited company profile.

2016

The beginning of JASO

JASO began as an electrical shop at Caravan Building, Plot 2B, Maruzi Road, opposite the UMEME Office in Lira City. The name was chosen to represent sweat, effort and the value of hard work.

2017

Company incorporation

On 23 May 2017, JASO Power Consult Limited was incorporated in Uganda as a company limited by shares under Section 18(3) of the Companies Act 2012. The company profile lists registration number 80020000259078.

2019

JASO Electric Mart opens

On 10 December 2019, the company opened JASO Electric Mart at Olet-Elyak Building, Plot 4, Maruzi Road, next to Olang Building, Lira City West Division. The profile identifies this as the main office.

2020

Working through the COVID-19 period

The company profile describes the challenges of operating during the COVID-19 era and credits the Board of Directors, management, sales team and technical personnel for helping the business continue through that period.

2022

Expansion to Gulu

On 24 October 2022, JASO officially opened another branch along Awere Road, opposite Gulu Main Market, Western Gate, next to Pentagon Suppliers, Gulu City. The branch provides sales, supply, installation, line construction and maintenance of electrical and solar appliances.

Continuing the work

JASO’s stated service areas include domestic wiring, electrical accessories, industrial and line construction, solar systems, installation, maintenance, consultancy, fabrication and technical training.
