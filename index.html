Who we are

Driven by hard work. Focused on quality.

JASO Power Consult Limited began in 2016 as an electrical shop at Caravan Building, Plot 2B, Maruzi Road, opposite the UMEME Office in Lira City. The name “JASO” reflects the Kiswahili word for sweat and the belief that hard work helps people achieve what they desire.

The company was incorporated in Uganda on 23 May 2017. In December 2019, it opened the JASO Electric Mart branch at Olet-Elyak Building on Maruzi Road in Lira City. In October 2022, it expanded to Gulu, extending its electrical and solar products and services.
